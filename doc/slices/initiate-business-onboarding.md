# Initiate business onboarding

- **Event model:** [04-business_onboarding.em.hcl](../event-model/04-business_onboarding.em.hcl)
- **Workflow:** `initiate_business_onboarding`
- **Tests:** [InitiateBusinessOnboarding tests](../../test/Tooyioo.Tests/Features/InitiateBusinessOnboarding)

## Decisions

- Business onboarding is initiated only by already onboarded users; the process belongs to a Tooyioo user.
- `InitiatedBy` records the app-owned `user_id`, not a Google-specific claim.
- The business onboarding id is generated by the command mapper, following the initiate user onboarding slice.
- Initiation writes only the business onboarding process stream. There is no separate claiming stream.

## Constraints

- Auth: the `UserRequired` policy requires the app-owned `user_id` claim.
- Idempotency: the command is not idempotent. Each request creates a new business onboarding.
- Single-stream consistency: the handler appends with the expected stream version. A concurrent append returns a conflict.

## Open questions

- Whether a user may have multiple concurrent business onboardings.
- Whether initiation should be idempotent per user or per business once a uniqueness rule exists.
